Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Hoju-106 (“the app”) does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

The app saves synthesizer patches, performance settings, and UI preferences in the app’s private storage on your device. You can remove this data by deleting the app or clearing its storage in iOS Settings.

Network

The app does not open network connections to send your data.

Bluetooth

The app uses Bluetooth only so you can pair Bluetooth Low Energy MIDI keyboards and controllers. The app does not collect or upload Bluetooth identifiers or location data.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from children.

Contact

Questions: scales-synth@protonmail.com
