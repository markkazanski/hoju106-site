Getting started

Audio starts automatically when you launch the app. Use the on-screen keyboard or switch to pads with the KEYS / PADS control.

MIDI

  • USB: Connect a class-compliant controller with a Lightning or USB-C adapter.
  • Bluetooth LE: Open MIDI in the app and pair from Bluetooth MIDI devices.

iPad keyboard shortcuts

Letter keys play notes; Z / X shift octave; Space toggles Hold.

Saving patches

Tap SAVE, enter a name, and confirm. User patches appear in the patch picker after factory presets.
